Notes
- `dojo.ogg` and `neon.ogg` are the OGG versions that OpenGameArt itself generated from the authors' uploads (a WAV and an MP3);
  the other three are the authors' own OGG files. The files are used unmodified (no re-encoding tools were available).
- Loop lengths: dojo 64 s, neon 17 s, rooftop 10 s, volcano 57 s, space 134 s. The player loops each file; whether the seam is
  perfectly clean has not been checked by ear.
- To change or add a track: put an OGG in this folder and edit `TRACKS` in js/audio.js. A missing file means that arena has no music.
